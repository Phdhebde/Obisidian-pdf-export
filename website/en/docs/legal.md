## Privacy

### The plugin

Report Exporter collects no data. It works entirely offline inside Obsidian: it makes no network request, has no telemetry and needs no account. Your notes, templates and exported files stay on your computer.

### This site

- **No cookies, no analytics, no advertising.** The publisher collects no personal data through this site.
- **Preferences in your browser.** The site remembers a few display preferences (for example light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are only used to display the site.
- **Fonts.** The fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) are served by this site itself; no request is made to Google Fonts or any other font service.
- **Hosting.** Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical data your browser sends when it loads a page, such as your IP address, and may keep it in its logs, for example for security. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Links to GitHub.** Opening an issue, a discussion or a pull request happens on GitHub, under [GitHub's terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service) and privacy statement.

### Your rights

As the publisher holds no personal data about visitors, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. For data held by GitHub, contact GitHub. You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority — in France, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr).
